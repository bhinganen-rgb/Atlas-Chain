Three principles
I

Attribution

Every claim carries the address of its submitter and the URL that occasioned it. Nothing enters the registry unattributed.

II

Adversarial review

Any visitor may challenge a claim in its opening window. Disputes are settled by a jury of stakers whose weight is their deposit.

III

Permanence

Verified claims persist. Rejected ones are catalogued, not erased — the record includes its own dissent.
